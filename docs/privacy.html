iROAR Premium

Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

iROAR Premium is an independent Chrome extension for students using Clemson iROAR.

Clemson pages and course planning

The extension reads service links, course sections and instructor names on supported Clemson pages to build the directory, class search and schedule preview. Search and registration requests use Clemson’s own systems. The preview stays in the current page and clears when you leave or reload it. The extension developer does not receive student records, sign-in credentials or schedules.

Instructor ratings and grade history

To look up a rating, the extension sends the instructor’s public name and Clemson’s school identifier to Rate My Professors. Like other web requests, the connection discloses your IP address to that service. The extension stores rating results and lookup timestamps in Chrome on your device. It reuses matched ratings for up to 24 hours and confirmed misses for up to two hours. Removing the extension clears its local storage. Published grade distributions are bundled with the extension and searched on your device.

Analytics and contact

The extension contains no advertising or usage analytics. The developer does not sell personal information. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
