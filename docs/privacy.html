iROAR Premium

Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

iROAR Premium is an independent Chrome extension for students using Clemson iROAR. It is not affiliated with Clemson University or Rate My Professors.

Clemson pages and course planning

The extension works only on Clemson’s iROAR dashboard and Clemson pages under sis.clemson.edu. It reads service links, course sections and instructor names on those pages to build the directory, class search and schedule preview. Search and registration requests use Clemson’s own systems. The preview stays in the current page and clears when you leave or reload it. The extension developer does not receive student records, sign-in credentials or schedules.

Instructor ratings and grade history

To look up a rating, the extension sends the instructor’s name as Clemson lists it and Clemson’s school identifier to Rate My Professors. When exactly one Clemson profile matches, it requests that profile’s rating summary. These requests do not include cookies, your Clemson account or other student information. Like other web requests, the connection discloses your IP address and browser details to that service.

The extension stores each result in Chrome’s extension storage on your device, labeled with the instructor’s name and the time of the lookup. A result holds the profile’s name, identifiers, rating, difficulty, number of ratings and would-take-again percentage, or a note that no single matching profile was found. The extension reuses matched ratings for up to 24 hours and confirmed misses for up to two hours. It replaces or deletes an expired entry the next time that instructor is looked up and does not store failed requests. Removing the extension clears its local storage.

Published grade distributions are bundled with the extension and searched on your device. Looking up grade history sends no network requests. Rate My Professors links open that website, which has its own privacy policy.

Limited use

The extension uses page content and instructor names only to provide the features above. It does not sell or transfer this information to anyone except Rate My Professors, as described, and does not use it for advertising or to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility. The developer does not collect or have access to it.

Analytics and contact

The extension contains no advertising or usage analytics. The developer does not sell personal information. Changes to this policy will appear on this page with a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
